### #4128: cross-site subframes on minor seats (2026-11-15, plan approved by the coordinator)

- **Rule.** On a minor seat a document of another origin than the top page it is drawn in loads only when `frameAllowed` admits it. That is the same admission as `allowlistOnly`: loopback or the trusted lesson allowlist. There is no dialog and no learner line: the learner did not ask for the frame, and an embedded ad would bring one each load. The host keeps counts only (`stats.framesRefused`). A refused frame fails with `BlockedByClient`, so it shows Chromium's blocked page and the page stays.
- **Where.** A subframe's first load is paused in its container's session (the probe on snts with `--site-per-process` showed this), and the guard decides it there. After that the frame is an out-of-process iframe: its later loads and redirect hops pause only in its own target. On a minor seat the page's auto-attach brings that target in paused, and the guard installs `Fetch.enable` (Documents) and its own `Target.setAutoAttach` before resuming it. So every later load is decided the same way, and the frame's own iframes, popups' iframes and nested frames are covered recursively. Same-origin frames, `srcdoc`, `about:blank` and `data:`/`blob:` frames are not network document requests to another origin, so they load.
- **Fail closed.** If a session cannot attach its out-of-process iframes, a cross-site frame's later loads could not be held. There a cross-site subframe is refused even when the list admits it; same-origin frames still load.
- **No network hold (verify-3928, 2026-11-15).** If a session rejects `Fetch.enable`, there is nothing to hold, so the fallback is the commit. Every session the guard watches (page, popup, out-of-process iframe) has `Page.enable`. A cross-site subframe whose `Page.frameNavigated` is off the list is sent back with `Page.navigate` to `about:blank`, by frameId, in its own session. It may show for a moment first, which is why the network hold is the rule. The host closes the tab with the `blocked` line in two cases: the frame cannot be sent back, or, with neither the hold nor iframe attach, a frame goes out of process (`frameDetached` with reason `swap`), where nothing can watch it.
- **Embeds (agent note).** On a minor seat an embed on an allowlisted page whose host is not on the list (a video player, for example) is blocked until the instructor adds that host. This is consistent with Jay's 2026-11-04 rule: kids' allowlists are gallery-only, and a site is added per lesson when its material needs it.
- **Adults.** Unchanged: frames load.
- **Accepted residual: subresources are not held** (images, scripts, styles, fonts, media, `fetch`, `sendBeacon`, WebSocket). Lesson sites and AI-made loopback pages load from CDNs, holding them would break lessons, and there is no confirm the learner could answer. A subresource can show content or send data out; it cannot move the learner to another page. The privacy-relevant part: on a minor seat, an AI-made loopback page could still send what the learner typed to a third-party host (`fetch` or a beacon). This is parked for Jay with three options, none built:
  1. a CDN host list;
  2. hold cross-site media only;
  3. block cross-site `fetch`, beacon and WebSocket only, on loopback pages only.
  - Confirmed by Jay: no. **Superseded 2026-11-15:** Jay chose option 3; see "Outbound requests from loopback pages" below.
- **Outbound requests from loopback pages (Jay, 2026-11-15).** Confirmed by Jay: yes (2026-11-15).
  - *Rule.* On minor seats, when the top document is loopback (an AI-made page), a cross-site `fetch`, XHR, `sendBeacon` (Ping) or EventSource is refused (`Fetch.failRequest`, `BlockedByClient`) unless loopback or the lesson list admits its address (`frameAllowed`).
  - *Unchanged.* Images, stylesheets, fonts, media and CDN scripts load as before. Pages on any other site, the lesson's allowlisted sites included, are not affected. Adults are not affected.
  - *Where.* The page, every popup and every out-of-process iframe session gets Fetch patterns for those types (Chromium reports `fetch` and EventSource as XHR) on minor seats only. A frame is judged by the top document it is drawn in.
  - *Notice.* One quiet line per tab per 10 s, no dialog: "이 페이지가 다른 사이트로 정보를 보내려 해서 막았어요."
  - *Without the network hold* (`Fetch.enable` rejected) there is no request-level fallback, so these requests are not held. This is documented, not coded.
  - *What "loopback" means* (verify-3928 on #4214; one helper, `isLoopbackHost`, for both the top-page check and the target check, and the same in core, #4216):
    - `localhost` and every `*.localhost` (RFC 6761), with or without a trailing dot;
    - 127.0.0.0/8 in any spelling the URL parser normalises;
    - `::1` and IPv4-mapped `::ffff:127.x.y.z`;
    - `0.0.0.0` and `::`, decided as loopback because Chromium sends them to this machine.
  - *Redirects.* A hop from a loopback or listed address to an off-list site is refused at the hop.
  - *Leaving the page.* A keepalive `fetch` or `sendBeacon` fired on `pagehide` arrives after the next page committed. Within 3 s of leaving a loopback page, a request that names no other initiator (its Origin or Referer is the old page's, or it has neither) is judged as the old page's and refused. The next page's own requests name it and are unaffected. Known corner (verify-r027 on #4222): if a confirmed off-list, non-loopback next page sends a same-origin fetch or XHR within those 3 s under a no-referrer policy (no Origin, no Referer), it is judged as the old page's and refused. That errs closed, and only on minor seats; next pages on the lesson list, assets and adults are unaffected. A possible later fix ties the judgement to the old document's loaderId or frameId instead of the window.
  - *Workers.* A dedicated worker's requests are paused in its page's session and held like the page's own. Shared and service workers are separate targets the guard does not attach: their requests are NOT held. This is an expected residual pinned by tests. Covering them in core (core's `webRequest` sees worker requests, together with xhr/ping) was the optional second commit of #4216; Jay did not approve it (2026-11-16), so it is parked on the branch `feat/4209-xhr-ping-core-later` and the residual stays documented and pinned.
  - *WebSocket: in Jay's scope, NOT yet covered.* Jay's 2026-11-15 decision includes WebSocket, but this slice does not hold it. The Fetch domain rejects a WebSocket resource type, and `Network.setBlockedURLs` did not stop the handshake in the snts probe.
    - No page-script `WebSocket` wrapper (coordinator, 2026-11-15): workers and service workers bypass it, and it would read as coverage it is not.
    - Fix: block ws/wss upgrades to off-list hosts for minor seats in every browser session's `webRequest`, through the core patch `scripts/patches/core/hps-safe-session.patch` (#4216, commit 1). **Confirmed by Jay: yes (2026-11-16), commit 1 only.** It reaches learners only with the next core-batch base rebuild; the submodule bump stays a separate, deliberate step.
    - Until that build ships, a test pins the residual as expected: a minor loopback page's WebSocket to an off-list host still connects in the extension-only build.
