### #4128: cross-site subframes on minor seats (2026-11-15, plan approved by the coordinator)

- **Rule.** On a minor seat a document of another origin than the top page it is drawn in loads only when `frameAllowed` admits it. That is the same admission as `allowlistOnly`: loopback or the trusted lesson allowlist. There is no dialog and no learner line: the learner did not ask for the frame, and an embedded ad would bring one each load. The host keeps counts only (`stats.framesRefused`). A refused frame fails with `BlockedByClient`, so it shows Chromium's blocked page and the page stays.
- **Where.** A subframe's first load is paused in its container's session (the probe on snts with `--site-per-process` showed this), and the guard decides it there. After that the frame is an out-of-process iframe: its later loads and redirect hops pause only in its own target. On a minor seat the page's auto-attach brings that target in paused, and the guard installs `Fetch.enable` (Documents) and its own `Target.setAutoAttach` before resuming it. So every later load is decided the same way, and the frame's own iframes, popups' iframes and nested frames are covered recursively. Same-origin frames, `srcdoc`, `about:blank` and `data:`/`blob:` frames are not network document requests to another origin, so they load.
- **Fail closed.** If a session cannot attach its out-of-process iframes, a cross-site frame's later loads could not be held. There a cross-site subframe is refused even when the list admits it; same-origin frames still load.
- **No network hold (verify-3928, 2026-11-15).** If a session rejects `Fetch.enable`, there is nothing to hold, so the fallback is the commit. Every session the guard watches (page, popup, out-of-process iframe) has `Page.enable`. A cross-site subframe whose `Page.frameNavigated` is off the list is sent back with `Page.navigate` to `about:blank`, by frameId, in its own session. It may show for a moment first, which is why the network hold is the rule. The host closes the tab with the `blocked` line in two cases: the frame cannot be sent back, or, with neither the hold nor iframe attach, a frame goes out of process (`frameDetached` with reason `swap`), where nothing can watch it.
- **Embeds (agent note).** On a minor seat an embed on an allowlisted page whose host is not on the list (a video player, for example) is blocked until the instructor adds that host. This is consistent with Jay's 2026-11-04 rule: kids' allowlists are gallery-only, and a site is added per lesson when its material needs it.
- **Adults.** Unchanged: frames load.
- **Accepted residual: subresources are not held** (images, scripts, styles, fonts, media, `fetch`, `sendBeacon`, WebSocket). Lesson sites and AI-made loopback pages load from CDNs, holding them would break lessons, and there is no confirm the learner could answer. A subresource can show content or send data out; it cannot move the learner to another page. The privacy-relevant part: on a minor seat, an AI-made loopback page could still send what the learner typed to a third-party host (`fetch` or a beacon). This is parked for Jay with three options, none built:
  1. a CDN host list;
  2. hold cross-site media only;
  3. block cross-site `fetch`, beacon and WebSocket only, on loopback pages only.
